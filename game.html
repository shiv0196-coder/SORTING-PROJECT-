⚠️ Chemical Safety

Chemicals are useful when handled correctly.

🧤

Use Protection

Use gloves, masks or other protective equipment when handling concentrated chemicals.

🏷️

Read Labels

Always read product labels, warnings, ingredients and usage instructions.

🚫

Never Mix Randomly

Never mix household cleaning chemicals unless the manufacturer specifically recommends it.

👶

Keep Away From Children

Store household chemicals and medicines safely away from children.

🌬️

Ventilation

Use strong cleaning products in a properly ventilated area.

♻️

Dispose Properly

Dispose of chemical containers and unused chemicals according to local instructions.
